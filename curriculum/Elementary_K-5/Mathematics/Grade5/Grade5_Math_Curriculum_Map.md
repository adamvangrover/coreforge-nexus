# Grade 5 Mathematics Curriculum Map

## Overview
This map outlines the sequence of units and lessons for Grade 5 Math, aligned with NYS Next Generation Learning Standards.

## Sequence

### Unit 1: Place Value and Decimals
*Focus: Understanding the structure of the base-ten system including decimals.*
- **Lesson 1**: Place Value to Billions and Thousandths (NY-5.NBT.1)
- **Lesson 2**: Powers of 10 and Exponents (NY-5.NBT.2)
- **Lesson 3**: Read, Write, and Compare Decimals (NY-5.NBT.3)
- **Lesson 4**: Rounding Decimals (NY-5.NBT.4)

### Unit 2: Operations with Whole Numbers
*Focus: Fluency with multi-digit multiplication and division.*
- **Lesson 1**: Multiplication Patterns
- **Lesson 2**: The Standard Algorithm for Multiplication (NY-5.NBT.5)
- **Lesson 3**: Division with 1-Digit Divisors
- **Lesson 4**: Division with 2-Digit Divisors (NY-5.NBT.6)

### Unit 3: Fractions
*Focus: Addition, Subtraction, and Multiplication.*
- **Lesson 1**: Equivalent Fractions
- **Lesson 2**: Adding Fractions with Unlike Denominators (NY-5.NF.1)
- **Lesson 3**: Subtracting Fractions with Unlike Denominators
- **Lesson 4**: Multiplying Fractions by Whole Numbers (NY-5.NF.4)

## Cross-Curricular Connections
- **Science**: Measuring volume of liquids (Unit 5).
- **Social Studies**: Analyzing population data using place value (Unit 1).
- **Art**: Geometric designs using coordinate planes (Unit 6).
