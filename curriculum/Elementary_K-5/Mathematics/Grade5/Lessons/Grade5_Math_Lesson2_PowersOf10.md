# Lesson 2: Powers of 10

## Lesson Overview
Students will explore the pattern of multiplying by 10, 100, 1000, etc., and learn to use exponents to denote powers of 10.

## Learning Objectives
1.  Explain patterns in the number of zeros of the product when multiplying a number by powers of 10.
2.  Use whole-number exponents to denote powers of 10 (e.g., $10^3 = 1000$).
3.  Multiplying decimals by powers of 10 (moving the decimal point).

## Procedure
1.  **The Pattern**:
    *   $3 \times 10 = 30$ (1 zero)
    *   $3 \times 100 = 300$ (2 zeros)
    *   $3 \times 1000 = 3000$ (3 zeros)
2.  **Exponents**:
    *   $10 = 10^1$
    *   $10 \times 10 = 100 = 10^2$ (2 factors of 10, 2 zeros).
    *   $10 \times 10 \times 10 = 1000 = 10^3$ (3 factors of 10, 3 zeros).
3.  **Moving the Decimal**:
    *   When multiplying by $10^2$ (100), the number gets bigger. The decimal point moves 2 spots to the *right*.
    *   $4.5 \times 10^2 = 450$.
    *   $0.325 \times 10^3 = 325$.

## Practice
*   Write $10,000$ as a power of 10. ($10^4$)
*   Solve $2.5 \times 10^2$. ($250$)
*   Solve $0.04 \times 10$. ($0.4$)

## Assessment
*   Fill in the blank: $10^5$ has _____ zeros.
*   Compute: $7.89 \times 10^3$.
