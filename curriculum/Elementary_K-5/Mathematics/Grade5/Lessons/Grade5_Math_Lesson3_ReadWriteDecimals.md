# Lesson 3: Reading, Writing, and Comparing Decimals

## Lesson Overview
Students will practice reading decimals to thousandths, writing them in standard, word, and expanded forms, and comparing them using inequality symbols.

## Learning Objectives
1.  Read and write decimals to thousandths.
2.  Write decimals in expanded form (e.g., $347.392 = 3 \times 100 + 4 \times 10 + 7 \times 1 + 3 \times (1/10) + 9 \times (1/100) + 2 \times (1/1000)$).
3.  Compare two decimals to thousandths based on meanings of the digits in each place.

## Procedure
1.  **Expanded Form**:
    *   Review whole number expanded form.
    *   Introduce decimal expanded form using fractions or decimals.
    *   $4.5 = 4 + 0.5$ OR $4 + \frac{5}{10}$.
2.  **Word Form**:
    *   The decimal point is "and".
    *   Read the number after the decimal as a whole number, then say the place value of the last digit.
    *   $0.34$: "Thirty-four hundredths".
3.  **Comparing**:
    *   Stack them!
    *   $4.05$
    *   $4.50$
    *   Start from left. Ones are same. Tenths: 0 vs 5. 5 is bigger. $4.5 > 4.05$.

## Activity: Decimal War
*   Students play in pairs with a deck of cards (Ace=1, 10=0, remove face cards).
*   Flip 3 cards to make a decimal (0. ___ ___ ___).
*   Compare. Higher decimal wins the round.

## Assessment
*   Write "Five and twenty-three thousandths" in standard form.
*   Compare $0.4$ and $0.399$. ($0.4 > 0.399$).
