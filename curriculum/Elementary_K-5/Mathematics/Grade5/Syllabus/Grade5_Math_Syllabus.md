# Grade 5 Mathematics Syllabus

## Course Overview
In Grade 5, instructional time focuses on three critical areas: (1) developing fluency with addition and subtraction of fractions, and developing understanding of the multiplication of fractions and of division of fractions in limited cases; (2) extending division to 2-digit divisors, integrating decimal fractions into the place value system and developing understanding of operations with decimals to hundredths, and developing fluency with whole number and decimal operations; and (3) developing understanding of volume.

## Learning Goals (NYS Next Generation Standards)
- **Number & Operations in Base Ten**: Understand the place value system; perform operations with multi-digit whole numbers and with decimals to hundredths.
- **Number & Operations - Fractions**: Use equivalent fractions as a strategy to add and subtract fractions; apply and extend previous understandings of multiplication and division.
- **Measurement & Data**: Geometric measurement: understand concepts of volume.
- **Geometry**: Graph points on the coordinate plane to solve real-world and mathematical problems.

## Weekly Schedule

| Unit | Weeks | Topic | Assessment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unit 1: Place Value & Decimals** | 1-5 | Powers of 10, Reading/Writing Decimals, Comparing, Rounding | Unit 1 Test |
| **Unit 2: Whole Number Operations** | 6-9 | Multiplication (Standard Algorithm), Division (2-digit divisors) | Unit 2 Test |
| **Unit 3: Fraction Operations** | 10-15 | Adding/Subtracting w/ Unlike Denominators, Multiplying Fractions | Unit 3 Test |
| **Unit 4: Decimals Operations** | 16-20 | Add, Subtract, Multiply, Divide Decimals | Unit 4 Test |
| **Unit 5: Volume & Measurement** | 21-25 | Volume formulas, Unit conversions | Unit 5 Test |
| **Unit 6: Geometry & Coordinate Plane** | 26-30 | Plotting points, Classifying 2D figures | Unit 6 Test |
| **Unit 7: Data & Statistics** | 31-33 | Line plots, Interpreting data | Unit 7 Test |
| **Review & Final Projects** | 34-36 | End of Year Review | Final Project |

## Assessment Policy
- **Weekly Quizzes**: Check for understanding of current topic.
- **Unit Tests**: End of unit summative assessments.
- **Performance Tasks**: Real-world application problems (e.g., "Design a Cereal Box" for volume).
