# Unit 1: Place Value and Decimals

## Overview and Alignment
This unit focuses on understanding the base-ten system, extending it to decimals up to thousandths. Students learn that a digit in one place represents 10 times as much as it represents in the place to its right and 1/10 of what it represents in the place to its left.

**NYS Next Generation Standards:**
- **NY-5.NBT.1**: Recognize that in a multi-digit number, a digit in one place represents 10 times as much as it represents in the place to its right and 1/10 of what it represents in the place to its left.
- **NY-5.NBT.3**: Read, write, and compare decimals to thousandths.

## Core Concepts / Units
1.  **Place Value Relationships**: The x10 and x1/10 pattern.
2.  **Powers of 10**: Using exponents to represent powers of 10 ($10^2, 10^3$).
3.  **Reading & Writing Decimals**: Standard form, Word form, and Expanded form.
4.  **Comparing Decimals**: Using <, >, and =.
5.  **Rounding Decimals**: Rounding to the nearest whole, tenth, or hundredth.
