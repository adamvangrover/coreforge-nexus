# Lesson 1: Fractions and Decimals

## Lesson Overview
A significant portion of the GED Math test involves operations with fractions and decimals without a calculator. This lesson reviews the basics of converting between forms and performing arithmetic operations.

## Learning Objectives
1.  Convert fractions to decimals and decimals to fractions.
2.  Add and subtract fractions with unlike denominators.
3.  Multiply and divide decimals.

## Procedure
1.  **Conversions**:
    *   *Fraction to Decimal*: Divide the numerator by the denominator (Top $\div$ Bottom). Example: $3/4 = 3 \div 4 = 0.75$.
    *   *Decimal to Fraction*: Read it! $0.5$ is "five tenths" -> $5/10$ -> simplify to $1/2$.
2.  **Adding Fractions**:
    *   **Rule**: You MUST have a common denominator.
    *   Example: $1/2 + 1/3$. Common Denom is 6.
    *   $3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6$.
3.  **Multiplying Decimals**:
    *   Multiply like normal whole numbers.
    *   Count total decimal places in the problem. Put that many in the answer.
    *   $0.2 \times 0.3 \rightarrow 2 \times 3 = 6$. Two decimal places -> $0.06$.

## GED Practice Question
*   *Question*: A carpenter cuts a board that is 10.5 feet long into 3 equal pieces. How long is each piece?
*   *Strategy*: $10.5 \div 3$.
    *   $10 \div 3 = 3$ with remainder.
    *   $10.5 / 3 = 3.5$.
    *   Answer: 3.5 feet.

## Assessment
*   Convert $0.4$ to a simplified fraction. (Answer: $2/5$).
*   Calculate $1/4 + 0.5$. (Answer: $0.75$ or $3/4$).
