# Lesson 1: The Scientific Method

## Lesson Overview
The GED Science test places a heavy emphasis on "Scientific Practices"—the ability to think like a scientist. This lesson covers the core process of scientific inquiry: asking questions, forming hypotheses, and designing valid experiments.

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
1.  List the steps of the Scientific Method.
2.  Differentiate between an **observation** and an **inference**.
3.  Identify the **Independent Variable**, **Dependent Variable**, and **Control Variables** in an experiment.
4.  Formulate a testable hypothesis.

## Key Vocabulary
*   **Hypothesis**: An educated guess or prediction (If... then... statement).
*   **Independent Variable (IV)**: The one thing you change (The Cause).
*   **Dependent Variable (DV)**: The thing you measure (The Effect).
*   **Control Group**: The group that does not receive the treatment (for comparison).
*   **Constants**: Things that stay the same to ensure a fair test.

## Instructional Procedure

### 1. Introduction: "The Broken Flashlight"
*   **Scenario**: You pick up a flashlight and turn it on. It doesn't work. What do you do?
*   **Steps**:
    1.  *Observation*: Flashlight doesn't work.
    2.  *Hypothesis 1*: The batteries are dead.
    3.  *Experiment*: Change batteries.
    4.  *Result*: Still doesn't work.
    5.  *Conclusion*: Hypothesis 1 was wrong. New Hypothesis: The bulb is burned out.
*   **Point**: We use the scientific method every day!

### 2. Identifying Variables (The "IV" and "DV")
*   **Mnemonic**:
    *   **I**ndependent: **I** change it.
    *   **D**ependent: **D**ata collected.
*   **Practice**: "A scientist studies how amount of sunlight affects plant growth."
    *   IV: Amount of Sunlight.
    *   DV: Plant Growth (height).
    *   Constants: Soil type, water, pot size.

### 3. GED Practice Question Analysis
*   *Question*: "A student wants to test if salt water freezes faster than fresh water. She puts two cups in the freezer. One has salt water, one has fresh water. What is the Independent Variable?"
*   *Discussion*: Why is it important to use the same type of cup? (Constant).

### 4. Activity: Design an Experiment
*   **Prompt**: Does chewing gum improve test scores?
*   **Group Task**: Design a simple experiment. Define the IV, DV, and Control Group.

## Assessment
*   **Exit Ticket**:
    1.  Write a hypothesis for the gum experiment.
    2.  Identify the Independent Variable in this sentence: "Does the weight of a paper airplane affect how far it flies?"
