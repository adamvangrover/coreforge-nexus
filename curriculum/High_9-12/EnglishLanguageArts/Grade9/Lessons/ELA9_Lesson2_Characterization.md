# Lesson 2: Characterization Analysis

## Lesson Overview
Students will dive deeper into character analysis using the STEAL method. They will practice identifying direct vs. indirect characterization using excerpts from "The Most Dangerous Game".

## Learning Objectives
1.  Distinguish between **Direct** and **Indirect** characterization.
2.  Use the **STEAL** acronym to analyze a character's traits.
3.  Write a character analysis paragraph using textual evidence.

## Materials
*   Text: "The Most Dangerous Game" (Excerpt).
*   STEAL Graphic Organizer.

## Procedure
1.  **Direct Instruction**: Define Direct vs. Indirect.
    *   *Direct*: "He was a mean man." (Tells you).
    *   *Indirect*: "He kicked the puppy." (Shows you he is mean).
    *   *Mnemonic*: **STEAL** (Speech, Thoughts, Effect, Actions, Looks).
2.  **Modeling**: Read the description of General Zaroff.
    *   *Looks*: "He had high cheekbones, a sharp nose, a spare, dark face..." -> Analysis: He looks aristocratic but severe/wolf-like.
    *   *Speech*: "I have but one passion: the hunt." -> Analysis: He is obsessed.
3.  **Group Work**: Assign each group a different character (Rainsford vs. Zaroff). Have them find one quote for each letter of STEAL.
4.  **Independent Writing**:
    *   Prompt: "Is General Zaroff a civilized man? Use one piece of evidence from the text to support your answer."

## Assessment
*   Check the STEAL charts for accuracy.
*   Review the paragraph for a clear claim and correct citation.
