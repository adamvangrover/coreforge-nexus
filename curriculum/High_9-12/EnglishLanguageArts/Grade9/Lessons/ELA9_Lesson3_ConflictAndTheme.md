# Lesson 3: Conflict and Theme

## Lesson Overview
Students will analyze "The Gift of the Magi" to identify the types of conflict present and derive the theme.

## Learning Objectives
1.  Identify Internal vs. External conflicts in a text.
2.  Differentiate between a **Topic** (one word) and a **Theme** (a full sentence message).
3.  Write a thematic statement.

## Procedure
1.  **Read Aloud**: "The Gift of the Magi" by O. Henry.
2.  **Conflict Hunt**:
    *   *Man vs. Society*: Poverty (External).
    *   *Man vs. Self*: Della worrying if Jim will like her hair (Internal).
3.  **Theme Formula**:
    *   Step 1: What is the topic? (Generosity, Love, Sacrifice).
    *   Step 2: What does the author say about the topic?
    *   *Draft*: "Sacrifice is good." (Too simple).
    *   *Refined*: "True love requires selfless sacrifice, which is more valuable than material possessions."

## Activity: Theme Posters
*   Small groups create a poster for the story with the Theme in the center and 3 pieces of textual evidence surrounding it.

## Assessment
*   What is the main irony in the story? (Situational Irony - they sold their most prized possessions to buy gifts for the other's prized possession).
*   Write one thematic statement for the story.
