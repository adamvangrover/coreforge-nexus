# English 9 Course Syllabus

## Course Overview
English 9 is a foundational high school course designed to develop students' critical thinking, reading, writing, and speaking skills. The curriculum explores the theme of "Coming of Age" through various genres including short stories, novels, poetry, and drama. Students will master the writing process, focusing on argumentative and narrative writing.

## Learning Goals (NYS Next Gen Standards)
- **Reading Literature**: Cite strong and thorough textual evidence to support analysis of what the text says explicitly as well as inferences drawn from the text.
- **Reading Informational Text**: Determine a central idea of a text and analyze its development.
- **Writing**: Write arguments to support claims in an analysis of substantive topics or texts.
- **Speaking & Listening**: Initiate and participate effectively in a range of collaborative discussions.

## Resources
- **Core Texts**: *Romeo and Juliet* (Shakespeare), *To Kill a Mockingbird* (Lee), *The Odyssey* (Homer), Selected Short Stories.
- **Writing Tools**: Graphic organizers, Rubrics for Argumentative/Narrative writing.

## Weekly Schedule

| Unit | Weeks | Topic | Core Text | Assessment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Unit 1: Short Stories** | 1-6 | Plot Structure, Characterization, Theme | "The Most Dangerous Game", "The Gift of the Magi" | Literary Analysis Essay |
| **Unit 2: The Epic Hero** | 7-12 | The Hero's Journey, Epic Similes | *The Odyssey* | Hero's Journey Project |
| **Unit 3: Drama & Tragedy** | 13-18 | Shakespearean Language, Dramatic Irony | *Romeo and Juliet* | Performance Task |
| **Unit 4: The Novel** | 19-26 | Social Justice, Symbolism, Perspective | *To Kill a Mockingbird* | Socratic Seminar & Argumentative Essay |
| **Unit 5: Poetry** | 27-30 | Figurative Language, Sound Devices | Selected Poems (Hughes, Frost, Dickinson) | Poetry Portfolio |
| **Unit 6: Research** | 31-35 | Evaluating Sources, Citations (MLA) | Non-fiction Articles | Research Paper |
| **Final Review** | 36-40 | Review of Concepts | All | Final Exam |

## Grading Policy
- **Classwork/Participation**: 15%
- **Essays/Projects**: 40%
- **Quizzes/Tests**: 30%
- **Homework**: 15%

## Expectations
Students are expected to read assigned texts outside of class to allow for deep discussion during class time. Plagiarism will result in a zero and a parent conference.
