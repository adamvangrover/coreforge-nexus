# Chapter 1: Elements of Fiction

## 1.1 Plot: The Skeleton of the Story
Plot is the sequence of events in a story. It is structured like a mountain (Freytag's Pyramid).
1.  **Exposition**: Introduces characters, setting, and the main situation.
2.  **Inciting Incident**: The event that sets the story in motion (The Hook).
3.  **Rising Action**: A series of complications that build suspense.
4.  **Climax**: The turning point; the moment of highest tension.
5.  **Falling Action**: The fallout from the climax.
6.  **Resolution (Denouement)**: How the story ends and problems are solved (or not).

## 1.2 Character: The Soul of the Story
Characters are the people (or animals/things) who drive the plot.
*   **Protagonist**: The main character.
*   **Antagonist**: The force opposing the protagonist.
*   **Static Character**: Stays the same throughout the story.
*   **Dynamic Character**: Changes/grows due to the story's events.

### Characterization (STEAL)
How do authors show us who a character is?
*   **S**peech: What do they say?
*   **T**houghts: What do they think?
*   **E**ffect on others: How do others react to them?
*   **A**ctions: What do they do?
*   **L**ooks: What do they look like?

## 1.3 Conflict: The Engine of the Story
Conflict is the struggle between opposing forces. Without conflict, there is no story.
*   **Internal Conflict**: Man vs. Self (A decision, guilt, fear).
*   **External Conflict**:
    *   Man vs. Man
    *   Man vs. Nature
    *   Man vs. Society

## 1.4 Theme: The Heart of the Story
The Theme is the universal message or lesson the author wants to convey. It is NOT just the topic.
*   *Topic*: Love.
*   *Theme*: Love can lead people to make irrational decisions.
