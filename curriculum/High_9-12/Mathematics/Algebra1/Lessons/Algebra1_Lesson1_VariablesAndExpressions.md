# Lesson 1: Variables and Expressions

## Lesson Overview
In this introductory lesson, students will transition from arithmetic to algebra by learning how to use variables to represent unknown quantities. They will translate verbal phrases into algebraic expressions and evaluate those expressions for given values.

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:
1.  Define "variable" and "algebraic expression".
2.  Translate verbal phrases into algebraic expressions (e.g., "five more than a number" -> $x + 5$).
3.  Evaluate algebraic expressions by substituting values for variables.

## Standards Alignment
- **NYS Next Gen AI-A.SSE.1**: Interpret expressions that represent a quantity in terms of its context.

## Materials Needed
- Whiteboard and markers
- Student notebooks
- "Translation Cards" (Index cards with math words like "sum", "product")
- Worksheet: "Translating Words to Math"

## Lesson Procedure

### 1. Introduction (5 mins)
- **Hook**: Ask students, "If a text message costs 10 cents, how much do 5 texts cost? 10 texts? 100 texts?" Write the pattern on the board: $0.10 \times 5$, $0.10 \times 10$.
- Ask: "How can we write a rule for *any* number of texts?" Introduce the variable $t$ for texts -> $0.10t$. This is an **algebraic expression**.

### 2. Direct Instruction (15 mins)
- Define **Variable** (a letter for a number) and **Expression** (math phrase with numbers, variables, operations).
- **Keyword Chart**: Create a chart on the board mapping words to operations:
    - Add: sum, plus, increased by, total
    - Subtract: difference, minus, decreased by, less than (**Caution**: "5 less than x" is $x - 5$, not $5 - x$)
    - Multiply: product, times, of
    - Divide: quotient, per
- **Modeling**: Walk through examples.
    - "The product of 4 and a number" -> $4x$
    - "Ten increased by a number" -> $10 + n$

### 3. Guided Practice (10 mins)
- Write phrases on the board. Have students write the expression on mini-whiteboards.
    - "A number squared plus 3" ($x^2 + 3$)
    - "The quotient of a number and 6" ($\frac{x}{6}$)
- Practice **Evaluating**: "Evaluate $3x + 2$ when $x = 4$."
    - Substitute: $3(4) + 2$
    - Solve: $12 + 2 = 14$

### 4. Independent Practice (10 mins)
- Students complete the "Translating Words to Math" worksheet.
- Problem set includes:
    1. Translate: "The difference of a number and 8."
    2. Evaluate: $5y - 3$ for $y = 2$.
    3. Word Problem: "Sarah is 3 years older than her brother. If her brother is $b$ years old, write an expression for Sarah's age."

### 5. Assessment & Wrap-up (5 mins)
- **Exit Ticket**:
    1. Write an expression for: "Twice a number decreased by 7."
    2. Evaluate $10 - x$ if $x = 3$.
