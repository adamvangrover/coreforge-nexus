# Lesson 2: Order of Operations and Real Numbers

## Lesson Overview
Students review the Order of Operations (PEMDAS) and apply it to algebraic expressions. They also classify real numbers into sets (Rational, Irrational, Integers).

## Learning Objectives
1.  Simplify expressions using the Order of Operations.
2.  Classify numbers as Rational or Irrational.
3.  Evaluate expressions containing exponents and grouping symbols.

## Key Concepts
*   **PEMDAS**:
    1.  **P**arentheses (Grouping Symbols)
    2.  **E**xponents
    3.  **M**ultiplication & **D**ivision (Left to Right)
    4.  **A**ddition & **S**ubtraction (Left to Right)
*   **Real Numbers**:
    *   *Rational*: Can be written as a fraction (1/2, 0.75, 5).
    *   *Irrational*: Cannot be written as a fraction ($\pi$, $\sqrt{2}$).

## Procedure
1.  **The "Facebook Math" Problem**: Put a viral math problem on the board (e.g., $6 \div 2(1+2)$). Poll students on the answer (1 or 9?). Reveal the correct answer using strict PEMDAS.
2.  **Complex Grouping**: Work through problems with nested brackets: $2[3 + 4(5-2)]$.
3.  **Substitution**: Evaluate $3x^2 - 5$ when $x = -2$.
    *   *Crucial Step*: ALWAYS use parentheses when substituting negative numbers! $3(-2)^2 - 5 \rightarrow 3(4) - 5 \rightarrow 7$. (Not $-12 - 5$).

## Practice
*   Evaluate $\frac{a^2 + b}{c}$ when $a=4, b=-2, c=7$.
*   Classify: Is $\sqrt{9}$ rational? (Yes, it's 3). Is $\sqrt{10}$ rational? (No).

## Assessment
*   Simplify: $10 - 2 \times 3 + 4$. (Answer: $10 - 6 + 4 = 8$).
