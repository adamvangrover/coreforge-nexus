# Lesson 3: Solving Linear Equations

## Lesson Overview
This lesson covers the core mechanics of solving linear equations: One-Step, Two-Step, and Multi-Step equations.

## Learning Objectives
1.  Solve one-step and two-step equations.
2.  Solve equations with variables on both sides.
3.  Identify equations with No Solution or Infinite Solutions.

## Procedure
1.  **The Goal**: Isolate the variable. "Get $x$ by itself."
2.  **Inverse Operations**:
    *   To undo $+$, we $-$.
    *   To undo $\times$, we $\div$.
3.  **The Two-Step Dance**:
    *   Step 1: Undo Addition/Subtraction (Move the constant).
    *   Step 2: Undo Multiplication/Division (Remove the coefficient).
    *   *Example*: $2x + 5 = 15$ -> Subtract 5 -> $2x = 10$ -> Divide by 2 -> $x = 5$.
4.  **Variables on Both Sides**:
    *   Strategy: Pick a side for the variable (usually the left, or the larger coefficient) and move the other one there.
    *   $3x + 4 = 5x - 6$ -> Subtract $3x$ -> $4 = 2x - 6$ -> Add 6 -> $10 = 2x$ -> $x = 5$.
5.  **Special Cases**:
    *   *Identity*: $2x + 1 = 2x + 1$ -> $0 = 0$ (True). All Real Numbers.
    *   *No Solution*: $2x + 5 = 2x + 1$ -> $5 = 1$ (False). No Solution.

## Common Mistakes
*   Forgetting to distribute the negative sign: $-(x + 3)$ becomes $-x - 3$.
*   Stopping at $-x = 5$ (Answer is $x = -5$).

## Assessment
*   Solve: $3(x - 2) = 12$.
*   Solve: $5x + 3 = 2x + 18$.
