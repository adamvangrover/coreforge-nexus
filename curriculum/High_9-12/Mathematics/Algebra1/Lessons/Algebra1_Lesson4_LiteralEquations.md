# Lesson 4: Literal Equations

## Lesson Overview
Students will learn how to rewrite equations and formulas to solve for a specific variable. This skill is essential for science (Physics formulas) and graphing linear equations ($y = mx + b$).

## Learning Objectives
1.  Define a **Literal Equation** (an equation with two or more variables).
2.  Solve a formula for a specific variable.
3.  Rewrite linear equations in function form ($y = ...$).

## Procedure
1.  **Analogy**: Compare solving $3x = 12$ to solving $ax = b$ for $x$.
    *   To get $x$ alone in the first one, we divide by 3.
    *   To get $x$ alone in the second one, we divide by $a$.
    *   Result: $x = \frac{b}{a}$.
2.  **Common Formulas**:
    *   Area of Rectangle: $A = lw$. Solve for $w$. (Divide by $l \rightarrow w = A/l$).
    *   Temperature: $F = \frac{9}{5}C + 32$. Solve for $C$.
        *   Subtract 32: $F - 32 = \frac{9}{5}C$.
        *   Multiply by Reciprocal: $\frac{5}{9}(F - 32) = C$.
3.  **Graphing Prep**:
    *   Solve $3x + 2y = 10$ for $y$.
    *   Subtract $3x$: $2y = -3x + 10$.
    *   Divide by 2: $y = -\frac{3}{2}x + 5$.

## Practice Problems
1.  Solve $D = rt$ for $r$.
2.  Solve $P = I^2R$ for $R$.
3.  Solve $x - 4y = 8$ for $y$.

## Assessment
*   Exit Ticket: Solve the perimeter formula $P = 2l + 2w$ for $l$.
