# Algebra 1 Course Syllabus

## Course Overview
This comprehensive Algebra 1 course is designed to meet the New York State Next Generation Mathematics Learning Standards. It provides a deep understanding of linear, exponential, and quadratic functions. Students will learn to model real-world situations, solve equations and inequalities, and analyze data. The course emphasizes problem-solving, reasoning, and communication.

## Course Goals & Learning Objectives
By the end of this course, students will be able to:
1.  **Interpret Structure**: Interpret the structure of expressions and write expressions in equivalent forms to solve problems.
2.  **Arithmetic with Polynomials**: Perform arithmetic operations on polynomials.
3.  **Equations & Inequalities**: Create equations that describe numbers or relationships and solve them.
4.  **Functions**: Understand the concept of a function and use function notation; analyze functions using different representations.
5.  **Linear, Quadratic, & Exponential Models**: Construct and compare linear, quadratic, and exponential models and solve problems.
