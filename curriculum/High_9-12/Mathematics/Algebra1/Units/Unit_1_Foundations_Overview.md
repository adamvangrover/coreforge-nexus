# Unit 1: Foundations of Algebra

## Overview and Alignment
This unit lays the groundwork for the study of Algebra. Students review and deepen their understanding of variables, expressions, and real numbers. The primary focus is on mastering the fundamental properties of arithmetic and applying them to solve linear equations, which is a critical skill for all subsequent units.

**NYS Next Generation Standards:**
- **AI-A.SSE.1**: Interpret expressions that represent a quantity in terms of its context.
- **AI-A.CED.1**: Create equations and inequalities in one variable to represent a real-world context.
- **AI-A.REI.3**: Solve linear equations and inequalities in one variable, including equations with coefficients represented by letters.

## Core Concepts / Units
1.  **Variables and Expressions**: Translating verbal phrases to algebraic expressions; evaluating expressions.
2.  **Real Number Operations**: Adding, subtracting, multiplying, and dividing integers and rational numbers.
3.  **Solving Linear Equations**: Isolating the variable using inverse operations (one-step, two-step, multi-step).
4.  **Absolute Value Equations**: Understanding distance from zero.

## Instructional Strategies & Resources
- **Direct Instruction**: Modeling the step-by-step process of solving equations ("Balance Scale" method).
- **Collaborative Learning**: Think-Pair-Share for translating word problems into equations.
- **Visual Aids**: Algebra Tiles to model combining like terms and solving equations.
- **Resources**:
    - *Textbook*: Chapter 1
    - *Lesson 1*: Variables and Expressions
    - *Lesson 3*: Solving Linear Equations

## Assessment Strategies
- **Formative**: Exit tickets (e.g., "Solve 2x + 5 = 15"), Whiteboard checks.
- **Summative**:
    - **Quiz 1**: Variables, Expressions, and Properties.
    - **Unit 1 Test**: Comprehensive assessment of solving equations.
