# Lesson 1: Introduction to Kinematics

## Lesson Overview
This lesson introduces the study of motion without regarding its causes. Students will learn the precise definitions of displacement, velocity, and acceleration, distinguishing them from distance and speed.

## Learning Objectives
1.  Differentiate between **distance** and **displacement**.
2.  Differentiate between **speed** and **velocity**.
3.  Define **acceleration** as the rate of change of velocity.
4.  Solve simple problems using $v = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}$ and $a = \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}$.

## Key Vocabulary
*   **Scalar**: A quantity with magnitude only (Distance, Speed).
*   **Vector**: A quantity with magnitude and direction (Displacement, Velocity, Acceleration).
*   **Displacement ($\Delta x$)**: Change in position ($x_f - x_i$).
*   **Average Velocity ($\bar{v}$)**: Displacement divided by time.

## Direct Instruction
1.  **Distance vs. Displacement**:
    *   *Demo*: Walk 3 steps right, then 2 steps left.
    *   Distance = 5 steps.
    *   Displacement = 1 step right (+1).
2.  **Speed vs. Velocity**:
    *   Speed is how fast you go. Velocity is how fast AND which way.
    *   Formula: $\bar{v} = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}$.
3.  **Acceleration**:
    *   It's NOT just "speeding up". It is *any* change in velocity (speeding up, slowing down, or turning).
    *   Formula: $a = \frac{v_f - v_i}{t}$.
    *   *Misconception Alert*: If acceleration is negative, are you slowing down? Not necessarily! You could be speeding up in the negative direction.

## Practice Problems
1.  A runner goes around a 400m track and finishes where she started. What is her distance? (400m). What is her displacement? (0m).
2.  A car goes from 0 to 60 mph in 5 seconds. What is its acceleration? ($12 \text{ mph/s}$).

## Assessment
*   **Exit Ticket**: A car travels 100 meters East, then 50 meters West. Calculate the total distance and total displacement.
