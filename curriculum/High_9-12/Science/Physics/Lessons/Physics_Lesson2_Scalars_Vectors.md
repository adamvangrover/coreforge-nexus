# Lesson 2: Scalars and Vectors

## Lesson Overview
Physics uses two types of numbers: Scalars and Vectors. This lesson focuses on graphical and algebraic representation of vectors, which is crucial for 2D motion.

## Learning Objectives
1.  Categorize quantities as scalar or vector.
2.  Represent vectors graphically (arrows).
3.  Add vectors in 1D (head-to-tail).

## Direct Instruction
1.  **Definitions**:
    *   **Scalar**: "How much?" (Mass, Time, Temperature, Energy).
    *   **Vector**: "How much and Which way?" (Force, Velocity, Momentum).
2.  **Vector Notation**:
    *   Represented by an arrow.
    *   Length = Magnitude.
    *   Arrowhead = Direction.
    *   Symbol: $\vec{v}$ or bold $\mathbf{v}$.
3.  **Adding Vectors (1D)**:
    *   Collinear: Simple addition.
    *   Opposite: Subtraction.
    *   *Resultant*: The sum of two or more vectors.

## Activity: Treasure Hunt
*   Write instructions using vectors: "Walk 5 meters North, then 3 meters East."
*   Students draw the path and find the "Resultant Displacement" (straight line from start to finish).

## Assessment
*   Quiz: Is "Temperature" a vector? (No). Is "Wind Velocity" a vector? (Yes).
