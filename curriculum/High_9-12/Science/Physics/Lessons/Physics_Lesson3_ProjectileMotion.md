# Lesson 3: Projectile Motion

## Lesson Overview
Students will analyze the motion of objects moving in two dimensions (projectiles). They will understand that horizontal and vertical motions are independent.

## Learning Objectives
1.  Explain why the horizontal velocity of a projectile is constant.
2.  Explain why the vertical velocity changes (-9.8 m/s²).
3.  Calculate the time of flight and range of a horizontally launched projectile.

## Demonstration: The Bullet Fired vs. Dropped
*   **Concept**: If you drop a bullet and fire a bullet horizontally from a gun at the same time, which hits the ground first?
*   **Answer**: They hit at the SAME time.
*   **Reason**: Gravity acts on both equally ($y$-direction). The horizontal motion ($x$-direction) doesn't fight gravity.

## Problem Solving Strategy
1.  **Make a T-Chart**: Separate X and Y variables.
    *   **X**: $v_x = ?, \Delta x = ?, t = ?$
    *   **Y**: $v_{iy} = 0$ (if horizontal launch), $a = -9.8, \Delta y = ?, t = ?$
2.  **The Bridge**: Time ($t$) is the same for both sides. Find $t$ using the vertical side, then use it on the horizontal side.

## Example Problem
A ball rolls off a 20m high cliff at 10 m/s.
1.  **Find Time**: Use $y = \frac{1}{2}gt^2$.
    *   $-20 = 0.5(-9.8)t^2$
    *   $t = \sqrt{40/9.8} \approx 2.02$ seconds.
2.  **Find Range**: $x = v_x t$.
    *   $x = 10(2.02) = 20.2$ meters.
