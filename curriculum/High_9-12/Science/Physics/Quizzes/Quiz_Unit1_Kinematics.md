# Quiz: Unit 1 Kinematics

**Name:** ____________________

**1. Definitions (2 pts each)**
Distinguish between the following pairs:
a. Distance vs. Displacement
b. Speed vs. Velocity

**2. Calculations (5 pts each)**
A cyclist rides 3 km East, then turns and rides 4 km North.
a. What is the total distance traveled?
b. What is the magnitude of the displacement? (Hint: Pythagorean theorem)

**3. Graphing (5 pts)**
Sketch a Position-Time graph for an object that is:
1. Moving forward at constant speed.
2. Stops for a while.
3. Moves backward twice as fast.

**4. Acceleration (5 pts)**
A car traveling at 20 m/s comes to a stop in 4 seconds. Calculate the acceleration.
