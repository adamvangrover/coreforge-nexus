# Chapter 2: Vectors and Two-Dimensional Motion

## 2.1 Vectors vs. Scalars
*   **Scalar**: Magnitude only. (Mass, Speed, Time).
*   **Vector**: Magnitude AND Direction. (Force, Velocity, Displacement).

### Vector Addition
*   Vectors are added "Tip-to-Tail".
*   The **Resultant** is the vector drawn from the tail of the first to the tip of the last.
*   **Pythagorean Theorem**: Used to find the magnitude of the resultant for perpendicular vectors ($a^2 + b^2 = c^2$).

## 2.2 Projectile Motion
A **Projectile** is an object upon which the only force is gravity.
*   **Horizontal Motion**: Constant velocity (no air resistance). $a_x = 0$.
*   **Vertical Motion**: Accelerated by gravity. $a_y = -9.8 \text{ m/s}^2$.

### The Key Concept
The horizontal and vertical motions are **independent** of each other. A ball dropped hits the ground at the same time as a ball fired horizontally from the same height.

## 2.3 Equations for Projectiles
*   **Horizontal ($x$)**:
    *   $x = v_x t$
*   **Vertical ($y$)**:
    *   $v_{fy} = v_{iy} + gt$
    *   $y = v_{iy}t + \frac{1}{2}gt^2$
